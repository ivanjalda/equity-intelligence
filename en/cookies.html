Cookie policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Right now this website does not install tracking, statistics or advertising cookies. That is why we do not show you any cookie notice.

What cookies are

They are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember you or measure your visit.

What exactly we use

WhatWhat forWhoDuration
Local storage: ei-temaRemember whether you prefer light mode or night modeThis website (it stays in your browser)Until you delete it

It is a preference that you choose and it is not used to track you or sent to any server.

Third-party services

The charts use a library served from jsDelivr (or cdnjs as a fallback). As far as we know, they do not install cookies in your browser. If that changed, we would say so here.

How to delete what is stored

In your browser settings, look for “Clear browsing data” and choose “Cookies and other site data”. You can also press the light/night mode button again, although that only changes the preference.

Changes

If in the future we add visit statistics or other services, we will update this page before activating them. Whenever possible, we will use tools that measure visits without cookies.
