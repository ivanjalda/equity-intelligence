Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

In short: you do not have to sign up to use the website. Only if you decide to create an account (with Google or with email and password) do we store your email and your list of favorite companies, so you can see it on any device. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Who is responsible

Community16 LLC, a limited liability company of New Mexico (USA), based in Albuquerque, New Mexico, USA. Contact: contacto@equityintrinsic.com

2. What data we process

If you create an account we process your email address and the list of companies you follow. If you sign in with Google, Google provides us with your email and your account identifier (we do not receive your Google password). If you use email and password, the password is stored encrypted and we cannot see it. If you also subscribe to the daily email summary, we use that email only to send it to you (market information and promotions for our own websites: Valmont & Co. and Nexus Terminal). Delivery is handled by Brevo, a service of Sendinblue SAS (France, EU), which stores your email for that purpose. You can unsubscribe with the link in every message. The website has no comments. This data is stored in Firebase, a service of Google LLC (USA).

Even so, when you visit any website, other companies that make it work may see technical data about your connection, such as your IP address and browser type:

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts the website and may log that technical data. Its policy: GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • News outlets. The photos of some news items are loaded directly from each outlet's servers, which can see your IP address and browser type. Each outlet has its own privacy policy. If you click a headline, you leave this website for the outlet's.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) sends the daily summary and stores the subscriber list. Its policy: Brevo privacy policy.
  • Google (Firebase) delivers the account code from its servers and stores your account and your favorites. Its policy: Google privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs (content delivery networks) deliver the library that draws the charts and may likewise see that technical data.

We do not receive those logs individually and do not use them to identify you.

3. What is stored in your browser

If you have an account, your browser also stores the signed-in session and your favorites so you do not have to sign in again. If you choose light mode or night mode, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage. It stays on your device, is not sent anywhere and you can delete it whenever you want. More details in the cookie policy.

4. What we use the data for and the legal basis

The legal basis for storing your account and your favorites is the performance of the service you request when you create it, and your consent when you accept this policy, which you can withdraw by deleting your account. The technical data seen by the services above is used to deliver the page to you (legitimate interest in the website working).

5. We do not sell or share data

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising (nor your email).

6. Your rights

If we were ever to process data about you, you would have the right to access it, correct it, delete it, object to its processing, restrict it and request its portability (EU General Data Protection Regulation and Spanish law). Residents of US states with a privacy law (for example, California) have equivalent rights. To exercise them, write to us at contacto@equityintrinsic.com.

If you live in Spain and believe we have not respected your rights, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

7. Minors

This website is not directed at minors.

8. Changes

If we add services that process personal data (for example, visit statistics or subscriptions), we will update this page before activating them and will say so here.

Information, not financial advice. Investing involves the risk of losing money.
